# Byte and Bolt

**Robotics Club | Ahmedabad University | Build • Test • Improve • Compete**

Byte and Bolt is a build event run by the Robotics Club. Every team builds a working combat robot prototype in 3 weeks, with a Team Leader, team members and a Mentor.

This is the official place for the rulebook, announcements and reports. **Anything posted here is part of the rules.** Check it every week.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Dates | 14 October to 4 November |
| Robot weight | Maximum 5 kg, with battery and weapon fitted |
| Budget | Rs. 2,200 per team. No overspending |
| Workspace | Room 308, GICT. Open 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM. **Must be empty 3:30 PM to 5:30 PM every day.** Clean your mess before you leave |
| Reports | Weekly report + progress meeting report twice a week |

## Start Here

1. Read the full rulebook: **[Byte_and_Bolt_Official_Rulebook.pdf](Byte_and_Bolt_Official_Rulebook.pdf)**. Every rule is mandatory.
2. Find your team's repository. You can only see your own team's repo.
3. Check [UPDATES.md](UPDATES.md) every week for rule changes, dates, and the final size, magnet and weapon limits.

## Who Does What

| Role | What they do on GitHub |
|---|---|
| **Mentor** | Reviews the team once a week. Verifies each report by commenting **"Verified"** on it |
| **Team Leader** | Posts the weekly report and the two progress meeting reports. Writes only what is really done |
| **Team Members** | View their team's repo. Tell the Team Leader about any problem |

## How to Submit a Report

1. Open your **team repository**.
2. Tap **Issues → New issue**.
3. Choose **Weekly Report** (once a week) or **Progress Meeting Report** (twice a week).
4. Fill in every section honestly. Planned work is not completed work.
5. Tap **Submit new issue**.
6. Your Mentor checks it and comments **"Verified"**.

### Deadlines
- **Weekly report:** end of every week.
- **Progress meeting report:** after each of the two meetings every week. Meeting days and times are posted in [UPDATES.md](UPDATES.md).

> Reports on GitHub do not replace bills. Stamped bills and the verified expense sheet are still handed to the Event Head every week (see Section 7 of the rulebook).

## Room 308 Rules

- Work time: **6:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight)**.
- **Empty from 3:30 PM to 5:30 PM every day.** Nobody stays in the room.
- Clean your own mess before you leave: scraps, packaging, tools, wires, dust. Tables and floor clean.
- Never leave batteries charging alone.
